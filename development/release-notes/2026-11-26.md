# Release Notes: 26 November 2026

## The Repeated Patterns of a Set of Words, as a Grammar

`formal` can now compress a set of words into a straight-line grammar by Re-Pair ([decision 0022](../decisions/0022-straight-line-grammars-by-re-pair.md)): the pair of adjacent letters that occurs most often becomes a new letter with its rule, again and again, until no pair occurs twice. The rules come out in the order of their frequency, nested, so they say what repeats in the words; every word is recovered exactly from the grammar.
