# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## Flows in Networks

Graphs can now carry flows ([decision 0019](../decisions/0019-flows-in-networks.md)).

- **The greatest flow and its least cut,** by Dinic's algorithm: the cut is the certificate that no flow is greater, and with unit capacities it counts edge-disjoint paths (Menger).
- **The cheapest flow,** by successive shortest paths with potentials, which certify it; with a price, a unit is shipped only while its path costs less, which gives Ford and Fulkerson's temporally repeated flows.
- **A flow as paths,** each with its amount.
- **Flows over time:** on the time-expanded network, the most that can reach the sinks by every step, with supplies released when they may move, waiting allowed and sinks of limited capacity. Checked against the temporally repeated flow at every horizon on random networks.
