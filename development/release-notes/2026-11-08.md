# 2026-11-08

## Centralities: Closeness and Betweenness Within a Radius

In this update morphe measures how central each vertex of a graph is ([decision 0012](../decisions/0012-centralities.md)): how near it lies to all others (total depth and harmonic closeness) and how many of the least paths between them pass through it (betweenness, by Brandes's algorithm), within a radius. Paths can be compared by one quantity and measured by another, as Space Syntax compares streets by the angles turned and measures them in metres, and costs of zero are allowed. It agrees with networkx on weighted graphs and with brute force on graphs whose ties and zero costs are many, and its sources can be spread over threads. urban science reads the configuration of streets through it.
