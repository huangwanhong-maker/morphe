# 2026-11-15

## Shortest Paths of Either Sign, and Formal Concepts

- **Weights of either sign.** Shortest paths may now be found when some weights are negative ([decision 0017](../decisions/0017-shortest-paths-of-either-sign.md)). Bellman–Ford names a negative cycle when there is one and otherwise gives potentials for the whole graph; Floyd–Warshall gives every pair at once. A set of bounds on differences of times is consistent exactly when its graph has no negative cycle, which is what the evidence foundation's timelines ask.
- **Formal contexts** ([decision 0018](../decisions/0018-formal-concepts.md)). Given objects and their attributes, one can list every concept (a set of objects with exactly the attributes they share) in lectic order. One can draw their lattice by its covering relation, and find the canonical basis of the implications that hold, the fewest from which all others follow. The case-oriented foundation zetesis reads a concept's intension and extension, and the ladder of abstraction, on it.
- **Logic** now has a home of its own: the planned `logic` row points to the foundation apodeixis ([decision 0016](../decisions/0016-logic-is-apodeixis.md)).
