# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 1. Regular Complexes and Their Rewriting, Traces, and Möbius Inversion

In this update a cell complex can be changed and stay a complex ([decision 0008](../decisions/0008-regular-complexes-rewriting-traces-and-mobius.md); the infrastructure's [ADR 0062](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0062-landscapes-as-cellular-configurations.md)).

- **Regular complexes.** A complex is given by its cells and their faces, and morphe decides whether it is regular: exactly up to dimension three, by homology above. Boxes of cubes, a building's rooms among them, are built directly.
- **Rewriting a complex.** A street can be cut at a point, a block split, two rooms merged, a wall turned into a door. Each change is a double pushout on the complex's incidence graph, refused with its reason if the result would not be regular, and it says what it read and wrote. Two complexes can be compared up to isomorphism.
- **The order of a record.** From what each event read and wrote, morphe gives the order the events must have had, the steps of events that could have happened at once, and how many orderings of the record are the same history.
- **Möbius inversion.** On any finite poset; on the subsets of a set of interventions it says what doing several together adds beyond each alone.
