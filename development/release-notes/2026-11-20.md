# Release Notes: 20 November 2026

## Normal Forms of Traces

Words over an alphabet in which some letters commute can now be compared up to those exchanges ([decision 0021](../decisions/0021-normal-forms-of-traces.md)). Each class of words has one representative, its least word in the order of the letters (Anisimov and Knuth's normal form), and a second form by steps of letters that commute (Foata's). Two words are the same up to exchanges exactly when their forms agree, and a separate test by the projection lemma confirms it. On every word up to length five over forty random alphabets, the forms match the classes found by making the exchanges one by one.

The first use is the infrastructure's search over orders of grants, which evaluates each order once up to exchanges of grants that commute.
