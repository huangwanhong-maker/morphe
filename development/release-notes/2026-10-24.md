# Release Notes: 24 October 2026

## 1. Persistent Homology

In this update morphe follows the homology of a complex as it is built cell by cell. Given a filtration, a stage for each cell with every face no later than its cofaces, it reduces the boundary matrix over ℤ/2 in the filtration's order and returns the intervals of persistence. Each interval is a class of H_k born at one stage and killed at a later one, or never, with the cells that create and kill it, and the Betti numbers at any stage. The infrastructure's history domain uses it to watch circles of encounters close and fill as a history unfolds in time (its [ADR 0041](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0041-the-history-domain.md)).
