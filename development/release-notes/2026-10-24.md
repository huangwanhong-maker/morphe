# Release Notes: 24 October 2026

## 1. Cycles, Q-Analysis and Canonical Forms

In this update morphe can compute three more things.

- **Cycles modulo 2.** It finds the cycles of a complex modulo 2, with a basis and the cells that lie in some cycle. In dimension 2 these are the spin foams of a ℤ₂ gauge theory, on non-orientable surfaces as on orientable ones.
- **Q-analysis.** It performs Atkin's Q-analysis of a relation read as a family of simplices: for each q, how many groups the simplices form when two belong together only through q + 1 shared vertices.
- **Canonical forms.** It writes any multigraph with labelled vertices and edges in a canonical form, equal exactly for isomorphic graphs, and counts its automorphisms; the Petersen graph's 120 are found where colour refinement alone sees nothing to split.

The infrastructure's history domain uses all three in place of its own code: the space of a history's spin foams, the Q-analysis of its occasions, and the types of the interaction vertices of its Feynman graph (its [ADR 0042](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0042-the-history-as-a-field-theory.md)).

## 2. Persistent Homology

In this update morphe follows the homology of a complex as it is built cell by cell. Given a filtration, a stage for each cell with every face no later than its cofaces, it reduces the boundary matrix over ℤ/2 in the filtration's order and returns the intervals of persistence. Each interval is a class of H_k born at one stage and killed at a later one, or never, with the cells that create and kill it, and the Betti numbers at any stage. The infrastructure's history domain uses it to watch circles of encounters close and fill as a history unfolds in time (its [ADR 0041](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0041-the-history-domain.md)).
