# Release Notes: 23 October 2026

## 1. Exact Algebra and the Topology of Complexes

In this update morphe has its first code ([decision 0004](../decisions/0004-the-first-slice-cell-complexes.md)). It computes exactly with integers of any size and with rationals, puts integer matrices in Smith normal form, and finds the homology of a chain complex together with cycles that generate it. It simplifies presentations of groups and counts the homomorphisms of a presented group into a finite one. It holds CW complexes with the words along which their 2-cells are attached, and simplicial complexes; builds surfaces from words, projective and lens spaces, products and lattice tori, presentation complexes and small triangulations; reads off the fundamental group, with a loop for each generator; tells which surface a complex is, whether a triangulation is a manifold and whether a 2-complex collapses (the dunce hat does not); and builds the dual 2-complex of a triangulation and the pseudomanifold that a coloured graph of a tensor model encodes, with its jackets and degree. Algebraic geometry is planned, with the order of its work.

## 2. A Wider Plan

In this update morphe's plan grows to cover logic and type theory, and combinatorics and graphs. Exact linear algebra is now named within algebra ([decision 0003](../decisions/0003-logic-and-combinatorics.md)). There is no code yet; the modules come when a domain needs them.
