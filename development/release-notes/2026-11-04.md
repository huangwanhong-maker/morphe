# Release Notes: 4 November 2026

## 1. Surfaces Given as Lists of Triangles

In this update morphe reads a surface given as a list of triangles, a mesh, directly as a 2-complex, and tells whether its triangles are consistently oriented ([decision 0010](../decisions/0010-surfaces-given-as-triangles.md)).

- **What one can now do.** Classify the surfaces of meshes with hundreds of thousands of triangles in seconds. Each component gets its type: a sphere, a torus, a disc, a Möbius strip, or not a surface and why (a pinch point). The complex is built by sorting its edges, not by closing the list under subsets.
- **Orientation kept.** Each triangle is attached in the order of its corners, so the classification also says whether that orientation is consistent. A surface must be consistent to bound a solid by the divergence theorem.
- **The same answers.** On every example the complex read directly agrees with the simplicial route in its cells, its Euler characteristic and its classification (10 new checks).
- **First use.** The infrastructure's anatomy domain classifies the 2,234 meshes of BodyParts3D's body with it; 2,201 are closed surfaces, consistently oriented ([ADR 0066](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0066-the-anatomy-domain-and-the-body-atlas.md)).

## 2. The Triangles of a Refined Worldline Complex, Found through Incidences

In this update the refined worldline complex finds the triangles of rungs at each event through its participants' incidences, rather than by trying every triple of the pairs meeting there. Each pair is followed to the pairs that share an end with it, and on to the one that closes the triangle.

- **What one can now do.** Build the refined complex of a record whose events gather thousands of pairs at once. A city's record of events is one: at the record's opening, every interface of a district meets, about two thousand of them. The triple loop, of the order of m³, could not reach such an event; the incidences reach it in seconds.
- **The same complex.** The triangles are those the triple loop found, in the same order, so every 2-cell keeps its index. A new check holds the two to each other on forty random records with parallel pairs and crowded events (146 triangles).
- **First use.** The infrastructure's urban-science domain builds a city's event complex on it ([ADR 0067](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0067-event-spacetime.md)).
