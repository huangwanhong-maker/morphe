# Release Notes: 4 November 2026

## 1. The Triangles of a Refined Worldline Complex, Found through Incidences

In this update the refined worldline complex finds the triangles of rungs at each event through its participants' incidences, rather than by trying every triple of the pairs meeting there. Each pair is followed to the pairs that share an end with it, and on to the one that closes the triangle.

- **What one can now do.** Build the refined complex of a record whose events gather thousands of pairs at once. A city's record of events is one: at the record's opening, every interface of a district meets, about two thousand of them. The triple loop, of the order of m³, could not reach such an event; the incidences reach it in seconds.
- **The same complex.** The triangles are those the triple loop found, in the same order, so every 2-cell keeps its index. A new check holds the two to each other on forty random records with parallel pairs and crowded events (146 triangles).
- **First use.** The infrastructure's urban-science domain builds a city's event complex on it ([ADR 0067](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/The-Science-Infrastructures/blob/main/development/decisions/0067-event-spacetime.md)).
