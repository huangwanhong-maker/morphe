# 2026-11-07

## Shortest Paths

In this update morphe finds shortest paths ([decision 0011](../decisions/0011-shortest-paths.md)): Dijkstra's algorithm on a weighted graph, from one source or several with offsets of their own, with a limit, giving each vertex its distance, its parent and the source nearest it. It is held to Manhattan distances on a grid and to Bellman–Ford on random graphs. Its first use is how far one can go over a city's ways, in the urban-science domain.
