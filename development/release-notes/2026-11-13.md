# 2026-11-13

## Braid Groups

morphe now computes with braids ([decision 0014](../decisions/0014-braid-groups.md)).

- **Words and what they show.** A braid is a word in Artin's generators. Its permutation, its exponent sum, which strands cross which, and the braid left when some strands are forgotten are read from the word.
- **The word problem, twice.** Two words are told the same braid or not by Dehornoy's handle reduction, which also orders braids. Artin's faithful action on the free group decides short words by another route, and the two are held to each other on random words of three to six strands.
- **Curves on a punctured disc.** A braid acts on Dynnikov's coordinates of a system of curves by rules of addition, maxima and minima, on integers without bound: a braid of a thousand letters acts in a thousand steps. The growth of the coordinates gives a braid's entropy, checked against two values known exactly.
- **Burau's representation** over the Laurent polynomials, which are new in `arithmetic`, and the Alexander polynomial of a braid's closure: the unknot, the Hopf link, the trefoil, the figure-eight knot and 5₁ come out as they should.

The conventions are fixed once: a word is read from the left in time, and a generator is the counterclockwise half-exchange, the left strand passing below. The infrastructure's new library kinesis reads braids from trajectories on these.
