# 2026-11-12

## Where Category Theory Lives

morphe now says where category theory is ([decision 0013](../decisions/0013-categories-as-objects-of-study-in-kategoria.md)). Computing with categories as objects of study (finite and presented categories, functors, limits, set-valued functors, monoidal theories, and the deciding of claims about them) is a library of its own, [kategoria](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/kategoria), which stands on morphe and uses its canonical forms of graphs, its grammars in BNF and its groups. morphe's own planned `categories` is narrowed to the constructions its modules share, and nothing of morphe moves.
